About the Client

The Alberta Equestrian Federation

The province's sole governing body for equestrian sport and industry, recognized by the Government of Alberta since 1978.

AEF serves nearly 15,000 members across 425+ Alberta communities, covering everything from competitive sport to recreational trail riding. Programs include coaching certification, Learn to Ride/Drive, Wild Rose competitions, trail initiatives, and equine welfare advocacy.

Every membership includes $5M liability insurance and $40K AD&D coverage, making it one of the highest-value equestrian memberships in the country.

We Understand the Challenge

The Barriers to Membership Growth

AEF doesn't have a competition problem. It has an awareness and clarity problem. We'll fix that.

Limited Awareness

Many equestrian participants across Alberta don't know AEF exists or what it does for them

Unclear Benefits

$5M liability insurance, $40K AD&D coverage, and funding programs aren't reaching the right people

"Not For Me" Perception

Recreational riders, trail riders, and hobbyists think AEF is only for competitive equestrians

Transactional Engagement

Members renew for insurance but don't feel connected to a community or movement

Our approach: reframe AEF membership from a transaction to a statement of identity. When you're part of the equestrian community in Alberta, AEF membership is what responsible participants do. Protection, value, and belonging.

Strategic Approach

One Campaign. Four Audience Pathways.

A unified message adapted for different motivations, all leading to the same outcome: AEF membership.

1

Barn Owners, Facilities & Coaches

High Priority / Multiplier Audience

One facility requiring AEF membership can bring in dozens of riders.

  • AEF membership protects your facility, students, and reputation
  • Professional liability framing, not fear-based
  • Toolkit assets for facilities to share with riders/boarders
2

Broader Equestrian Community

Recreational & Non-Competitive

Trail riders and hobbyists who don't realize AEF is for everyone.

  • Normalize AEF membership as part of riding in Alberta
  • Plain-language benefit breakdowns and community stories
  • "I ride in Alberta. I'm AEF." identity-driven messaging
3

Families, Youth & New Riders

Growth Segment

Parents need reassurance. New riders need structure. AEF provides both.

  • Safety-first messaging: insurance as peace of mind
  • Highlight Learn to Ride/Drive and coaching pathways
  • Welcoming tone that removes intimidation
4

Lapsed & Inactive Members

Re-engagement

They said yes once. We remind them why.

  • "Welcome back" email sequences and retargeting
  • What's new since they last engaged
  • Community-driven "you're missed" social proof

How We Talk About Insurance (Without Fear)

Insurance is the #1 motivator, but fear-based messaging is off the table. We agree. Here's how we reframe it:

❌ Not This

"Without insurance, one accident could cost you everything."

✅ This

"Responsible riders ride protected. AEF membership means $5M in liability coverage, so you can focus on what you love."

Insurance becomes a badge of responsibility, not a reaction to risk. It's what smart, committed equestrians do.
